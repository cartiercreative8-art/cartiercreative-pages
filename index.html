How it works

What happens when you can't pick up.

You don't learn new software. Your phone keeps ringing like it does now. The system works the calls you can't get to.

  1. 01 · Seconds later

    They get a text back

    Every missed call gets a text from your business number. The customer feels answered, so they don't call the next name on Google.

  2. 02 · Same conversation

    The job gets booked

    A review request goes out on its own. Follow-ups go to people who called but didn't book. More reviews bring more calls.
