Questions

Things owners ask.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

No. If your site works, we connect the system to it. If you don't have one, or it's not bringing in calls, Core includes a simple site with chat built in.

What does the Pro receptionist actually do?

It picks up when you can't. It asks what the customer needs, answers basic questions about your business, and books the job on your calendar. You get the details by text.

Is it a real person on the phone?

No. It's an automated receptionist set up with your business name, your services, and your schedule. Callers get a quick, friendly answer instead of voicemail. If someone wants you, it takes a message and texts you right away.

What's the free audit?

A 30-minute call. I look at your Google profile, your reviews, your website, and what happens when someone calls and you can't answer. You get a short list of what's costing you jobs. It's yours to keep, even if you never sign up.

Who sets it all up?

I do. You answer some questions about your business and your schedule. I build it, test it, and walk you through it before it goes live.

I'm not a plumber or HVAC shop. Is this for me?

If you're the owner, you're on the job, and you're the one answering the phone, yes. Roofing, electrical, landscaping, snow removal, handyman. Snow crews can also see the snow route offer.
